# KTPeach Hurricane Relief System

This project will develop a digital platform to help communities access and coordinate hurricane-relief resources. During and after a hurricane, information about shelters, food, medical care, supplies, and volunteer support is often scattered across many sources, making it difficult for people to quickly find the help they need. Our product will provide a centralized, easy-to-use place for affected residents to find reliable resources and for relief organizations or volunteers to better identify community needs. The goal is to improve communication, reduce confusion, and help support a faster, more organized recovery process.
